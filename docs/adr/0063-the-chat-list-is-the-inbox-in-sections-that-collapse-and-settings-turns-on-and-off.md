# The chat list is the Inbox, in sections that collapse and that Settings turns on and off

> **Partly superseded by [ADR 0090](0090-clearing-a-chat-from-the-inbox-settles-it-and-settled-is-a-drawer-at-the-lists-foot.md)** (#278). Clearing a chat from the Inbox now settles it: the Unsettled section, Clear from the Inbox and Back to the Inbox are gone. Settled is a drawer at the list's foot again, so "Settled leaves the foot of the list" no longer holds. The rest of this ADR stands.

Issue #219. The chat list beside the chat (the issue calls it the side panel; GLOSSARY's **Side panel** is the panel on the other side, with Files and the Terminal) had an unlabeled top of chats that need the owner or are done, then Working, a collapsed Snoozed section, and Settled as a drawer at the list's foot (ADR 0002, ADR 0036). Archived chats were a separate view behind an eye button. Every Active chat that wasn't working or snoozed stayed at the top until settled, however long ago it went quiet, so the top filled with chats that needed nothing.

## Decisions

- **The list is six sections, in this order: Inbox, Working, Snoozed, Unsettled, Settled, Archived.** Each chat is in exactly one (snooze.ts `chatSections`). The Inbox is what was the top: Active chats that need the owner or are done, newest first. Unsettled holds the Active chats the owner cleared from the Inbox.
- **Clear from the Inbox, not "close".** The issue's word is close, but Close already closes tabs and panes, and GLOSSARY keeps "closed" away from Settled. The row's button and its right-click menu say **Clear from the Inbox**, and **Back to the Inbox** on a cleared chat. Clear is offered only on a read, quiet Active chat: not Unread, no reply running, Hermes not waiting on the owner.
- **A clear holds until something newer happens in the chat.** It is kept with when the chat was last active as the list counts it (snooze.ts `lastActive`: Hermes's own activity time, a snooze that woke, a reply this app saw stop). Anything later, from any platform, puts the chat back in the Inbox; so does it turning Unread or Hermes waiting on the owner. Nothing is deleted and nothing goes to Hermes: clears are kept on this computer only, for each connection and profile, in their own localStorage document (`hermes-chat.cleared`), as snoozes are (ADR 0036). A clear that has ended is forgotten, not just passed over, so the chat stays in the Inbox once the activity that ended it stops counting (a woken snooze does after a week). Settling, unsettling, archiving, deleting or snoozing the chat forgets its clear too, so an unsettled chat comes back to the Inbox; so does a refresh that finds the chat Settled or Archived, which another Hermes client may have done without changing when the chat was last active.
- **Every section's header collapses it, remembered across restarts.** The header shows the section's name and count, and a dot when the section is collapsed and a chat in it is Unread. Which sections are collapsed is a display preference (uiPrefs.ts `chatSectionsCollapsed`). Inbox and Working start open; Snoozed, Unsettled, Settled and Archived start collapsed, so old quiet chats are out of view until asked for. Opening a chat from elsewhere (a search, a link) opens its section, as opening a Settled chat opened the drawer.
- **Settings → General, Chats, picks the sections shown.** A **Chat list sections** card has a switch for each. Any mix works. The last one on can't be turned off: its switch is disabled and says at least one section stays on, and the stored preference (`chatSectionsShown`) refuses an empty list too. Search (Ctrl+K) asks Hermes, not the list, so it still finds the chats in a hidden section. All sections show except Archived until the owner changes it.
- **Snoozed is its own section, with its own switch.** Working means a reply is running, and its chats leave it on their own; a snooze is the owner's choice and can last a year. Folding one into the other would make Working's count mean two things, and hiding Working would hide snoozes.
- **Archived is a section, and the eye button goes.** Archived chats are fetched with each refresh only while the Archived section is shown, as the archived view fetched them only while open (`listArchived`), which is why it starts off.
- **Settled leaves the foot of the list.** It is a section in the list's scroll like the others. A drawer with its own scroll inside the list worked for one section; six can't each have one.
- **The rail's Inbox button stays the Rail inbox.** It lists what needs the owner across every profile on the server, chats in any state, with its count; the list shows one profile's chats. GLOSSARY now calls it the **Rail inbox** to keep it apart from the list's **Inbox**. Clearing a chat doesn't dismiss its Rail inbox entry, and dismissing an entry doesn't clear the chat.
- **What already worked stays.** Unread dots, right-click menus, Settle and Unsettle, dragging a row to a pane to split, Up and Down between the rows on screen, and Alt+Up and Alt+Down through the Inbox and Working.
- **A collapsed section that holds Active chats keeps its rows, hidden**, so a row that moves into it (a reply starts, another window snoozes it) keeps its rename box and menu, as Snoozed did (ADR 0036). Collapsed Settled and Archived draw no rows: they can hold every chat the profile has had. Working and Snoozed draw no header while empty; the others keep theirs, and an open empty one says so.

## Considered options

- **Clearing read chats on their own after some days.** The issue asks for chats the owner closes. Chats leaving on a timer would make the Inbox's contents depend on when the app last looked. A setting for it can come later on top of the same store.
- **An Inbox of only Unread and waiting chats.** Opening a chat to glance at it would take it out of the Inbox at once, and "a reply done" would vanish as soon as it was read.
- **The rail's Inbox button opening the list's Inbox section.** The list shows one profile; the button's list and count cover every profile, which is what it is for.
- **Sharing the Rail inbox's dismissals.** Those are per connection and per event (an Approval, a failure); a clear is per chat in one profile, and lasts until anything newer happens.
- **Keeping Settled as a drawer at the foot.** See above: one rule for every section.

## Consequences

- **ADR 0050** (the rail): the Inbox button at the rail's top is the Rail inbox; the button, its count and its tip are unchanged.
- **ADR 0116** (the Side panel and the Inbox closing on a click away): the Side panel is unchanged. "The Inbox" there is the Rail inbox, which still closes on a press outside it.
- **ADR 0036**: Snoozed is one of six sections, and its collapsed state is now remembered. The top it names is the Inbox.
- **ADR 0002**: Settled chats are in a collapsible Settled section, not a drawer at the foot.
- While the Archived section is hidden, archived chats aren't in the list, so a chat archived and then restored in another Hermes client in that time keeps its clear and comes back to Unsettled. Forgetting the clears of chats missing from the list would cover it, but would also forget every clear whenever the list is briefly empty or partial (a first sign-in, a refresh that failed), so it isn't done.
- Tests find a section by its header button (its name and count), and its rows by `data-section` (`inbox`, `working`, `snoozed`, `unsettled`, `settled`, `archived`).
